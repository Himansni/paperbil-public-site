Privacy Policy.

How PaperBil handles information used to provide newspaper agency management workflows.

Publication status: draft for review. This page is not a final legal notice. Verify the production app's actual permissions, data flows, service providers, retention controls and contact channels before publishing it as binding policy.

Brand: PaperBil
Last updated: October 2026 (draft)

1. About this policy

PaperBil is designed to help newspaper agencies manage customer records, subscriptions, delivery arrangements, billing, collections and employee access. The agency using PaperBil is responsible for the customer and employee information it enters and for providing any notices or obtaining any permissions required for its use.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features used, information may include agency name and contact details; employee account and role information; customer names, contact details, delivery instructions and subscriptions; newspaper rates; bills, payment records, adjustments and audit history; and authentication, security and consent records.

3. Location information

The policy must accurately distinguish device location permissions from any manually entered address or delivery note. Do not claim that PaperBil never accesses location until the released app's permissions and code have been verified. This draft makes no claim that GPS or background-location data is collected or not collected.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate users, separate agency workspaces, manage customers and delivery workflows, calculate and record bills, track collections, provide reports, support customer requests, protect service integrity and comply with applicable obligations.

5. Service providers

The final notice should identify the providers actually used in production, the purposes for which they process data, relevant hosting regions, and any payment processor involved in subscription payments. Verify current Firebase/Google and payment integration behavior before publication.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and personal information may be removed or anonymized when a verified deletion request is completed, subject to applicable obligations and the need to preserve legitimate accounting, transaction, security or audit records. The final policy must specify actual retention practices and timelines rather than imply that all records are immediately erased.

7. Security

PaperBil should use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your requests and contact

For account deletion, see the account deletion page. For privacy questions, contact PaperBil support using the currently verified contact channel.

9. Changes

This notice should be updated when product behavior, service providers or legal requirements materially change.
