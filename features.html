PAPERBIL FEATURES

The work behind your agency, better organized.

Explore the core workflows PaperBil is designed to bring together. Available functionality can vary by app version and plan.

01

Customer management

Maintain customer profiles, contact details, delivery instructions, subscription selections and account status in one place.

02

Daily delivery workflow

Organize customers by delivery area and sequence. Support the daily work of planning and managing newspaper distribution.

03

Subscriptions and billing

Maintain newspaper rates and subscription changes, account for pauses, and prepare and review monthly bills.

04

Collections and payments

Record collections, allocate payments against bills and review balances. Payment records should reflect confirmed agency actions.

05

Employee access

Support team access through roles and assigned delivery areas, helping agency owners coordinate work.

06

Reports and exports

Review available billing, collections, outstanding-balance and customer reports, with supported data exports.

Product accuracy matters. This page describes intended core workflows, not a guarantee that every feature is enabled for every account. Confirm current plan limits and availability before publishing final commercial claims.
