ACCOUNT & DATA

Request account deletion.

You can request deletion of your PaperBil account and associated personal information through a verified process.

Important: request flow not yet connected. This page is an informational draft. It does not submit or execute a deletion request. Do not publish it as a functioning deletion endpoint until the secure verification and backend workflow are implemented and tested.

How the request should work

  1. Submit a request through the official, verified PaperBil deletion channel.
  2. Complete the identity-verification steps provided to protect your account.
  3. PaperBil reviews the request and any agency closure requirements that apply.
  4. When the request is processed, personal information is deleted or anonymized where appropriate, subject to lawful retention needs.

What may be retained?

Some billing, payment, reversal, security or audit records may need to be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal obligations. Where appropriate, personal identifiers may be removed or restricted while historical records are preserved.

Agency owner requests

Closing an agency may require resolving active delivery routes, employee memberships and other dependent records before closure can be completed. The final workflow should explain these requirements and offer clear next steps.

Need help?

Use the verified contact information on the Support page. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or payment credentials by email or chat.
