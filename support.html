What to include when reaching out

To help us assist your agency as quickly as possible, please include:

  • Agency details: Your agency name and town or city location.
  • Contact information: Your name and registered mobile phone number.
  • Help needed: A brief description of your question or issue (for example: new agency setup, delivery route sequences, monthly bill generation, plan change, or account closure).

Security warning: Never send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, banking credentials, or customer payment card numbers. PaperBil support will never ask you for your account password or banking PIN.
